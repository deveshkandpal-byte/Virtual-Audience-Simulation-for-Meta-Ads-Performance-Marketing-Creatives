# Meta Ads Industry Strategy Skill

## References
Read docs/INDUSTRY_STRATEGY_PLAYBOOK.md, app/industry_strategies.json, docs/CREATIVE_TYPE_LIBRARY.md and prompts/SYSTEM_PROMPT.md when available.

## Workflow
1. Identify product/service, industry, objective, audience, funnel stage, offer, CTA, creative and destination.
2. Classify industry using explicit input or accessible landing-page evidence; include confidence. Use general guidance if uncertain.
3. Audit only supplied media and extracted landing-page evidence. Mark inaccessible or untested items unknown.
4. Select relevant formats and distinct messaging angles. Recommend hooks/headlines, copy structure, CTA and offer tests suited to industry and funnel.
5. Keep facts, claims, offers, testimonials and terms grounded in evidence; use placeholders for missing details.
6. Simulate cold, warm and high-intent reactions as hypotheses, not observed responses.
7. Return prioritized modifications, risk flags, evidence gaps and a controlled test plan tied to the campaign objective.
8. If a provider is configured, draft up to three distinct variants; otherwise provide local guidance and disclose the limitation.

## Integrity
Do not claim exact CTR/CPA/ROAS or a winning creative without validated campaign data. Do not invent prices, scarcity, reviews, certifications, results or guarantees. Do not claim Meta-policy approval. Use caution for regulated/sensitive categories. Never publish campaigns or spend budget automatically.
