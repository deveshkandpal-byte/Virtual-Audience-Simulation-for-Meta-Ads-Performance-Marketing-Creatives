# Creative type and execution-style library

This library follows the project's Master Prompt. These are formats, concepts, and execution styles—not 30 mutually exclusive categories. One ad may combine several styles. Choose only relevant styles; never force every format into every campaign.

## A. Static image ads
- Single static image
- Product hero shot
- Lifestyle image ad
- Offer or discount graphic
- Problem–solution image
- Before-and-after visual
- Infographic or educational graphic
- Review or rating image
- Founder or brand image

## B. Video ads
- Close-up or macro product video
- Product demonstration video
- Unboxing video
- ASMR video
- Lifestyle or cinematic commercial
- Problem–solution video
- Before-and-after video
- How-to or tutorial video
- Founder-led brand video
- Animation or motion graphics
- 3D product video

## C. UGC-style ads
- Talking-head UGC
- Customer experience story
- Product review
- Unboxing and first impressions
- Product routine or usage demonstration
- Testimonial-style UGC
- UGC voice-over with product footage
- Creator-led problem–solution story
- Influencer-style recommendation

Distinguish genuine customer-created content from commissioned creator content presented in a UGC style. Never assume a testimonial or result is genuine without evidence.

## D. Carousel ads
- Multiple-product showcase
- Product features explained card by card
- Step-by-step educational carousel
- Problem → solution → benefit carousel
- Different product variants
- Customer reviews across cards
- Service showcase carousel
- Storytelling carousel
- Product or service comparison carousel

Review first-card stopping power, card order, narrative continuity, visual consistency, swipe motivation, and the final conversion message.

## E. Collection and catalog ads
- Collection ad with a hero creative
- Product catalog ads
- Dynamic product retargeting
- Personalized product recommendations
- Product-set or category ads

Review catalog relevance, product consistency, product discovery, shopping experience, and campaign/catalog eligibility where known.

## F. Testimonial and social proof
- Customer testimonial
- Customer review or rating
- Influencer or creator endorsement
- Founder story
- Expert-led advertisement
- Podcast-style advertisement
- Street interview or vox-pop
- Challenge or experiment-based creative

Review authenticity, evidence, relevance, credibility, permissions, and objection handling. Do not fabricate endorsements, customer identity, or outcomes.

## G. Comparison and education
- Product or service comparison
- Competitor-alternative comparison
- Objection-handling ad
- FAQ-based ad
- Feature and benefit explainer
- Demonstration with text overlays
- Tutorial or educational content
- Screen-recording advertisement
- Animated explainer

Review factual accuracy, differentiation, clarity, usefulness, and ease of understanding.

## H. Promotional and conversion-focused
- Offer-led advertisement
- Discount or sale advertisement
- Limited-time promotion
- Bundle or package offer
- Free trial or sample offer
- Lead magnet advertisement
- Free audit or consultation offer
- WhatsApp conversation advertisement
- Direct-response advertisement
- Retargeting creative
- Personalized catalog creative

Review value, offer clarity, substantiation, urgency, trust, friction, CTA alignment, and destination. Never invent discounts, guarantees, availability, or deadlines.

## I. Storytelling and engagement
- Brand storytelling
- Customer transformation story
- Relatable humor or meme creative
- Trend-based creative
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Day-in-the-life content
- Lifestyle demonstration
- Product discovery or reveal
- Unboxing or packaging reveal
- Local business advertisement

Review emotional relevance, audience identification, attention, message retention, and the connection to the actual offer.

## J. Specialized / industry-specific styles
- Food close-ups, food preparation, texture shots, and ASMR
- Property walkthroughs, room-by-room showcases, and floor-plan creatives
- Beauty and personal-care routines
- Fashion try-ons and styling demonstrations
- Software and app interface demonstrations
- Service-business case studies
- Local business location and service showcases
- Product assembly or usage demonstrations
- Expert explanations for complex offerings
- Lead-generation creatives for service businesses

## Required component checks
For every relevant creative, assess:
- Heading and hook
- Primary ad copy and message clarity
- Offer and evidence supporting claims
- CTA and conversion destination
- Visual hierarchy, composition, readability, brand consistency, and product visibility
- Trust signals and audience–creative fit
- Funnel stage, objections, intent, and campaign objective
- Landing page/destination consistency and conversion friction where information is available

Format-specific checks:
- Video: first seconds, hook, pacing, subtitles, audio, voice-over, demonstration, closing CTA. If only text is supplied, label this a script/concept review; do not claim to have analyzed the actual video.
- Carousel: first card, card sequence, swipe motivation, consistency, final CTA.
- Catalog/collection: catalog and product relevance, imagery, shopping transition.
- UGC/testimonial: natural delivery, credibility, and evidence for claims.
- Image: visual checks only when an image is actually inspected by a vision-capable provider; otherwise disclose the limitation.

## Audience and output requirements
Where useful, differentiate cold, problem-aware, solution-aware, product-aware, warm, and high-intent audiences; include relevant funnel stages (TOFU/MOFU/BOFU). State assumptions and uncertainty. Assess engagement, CTR, and conversion qualitatively unless a defensible, validated quantitative basis exists. Never imply these are live Meta Ads results.

The structured report should preserve the supplied components, identify strengths and weaknesses, give specific component-level recommendations, use one of the Master Prompt launch-readiness labels, include a comparative summary for multiple creatives, and provide a three-level prioritized action plan. Missing facts must be marked "Not provided", not invented.
