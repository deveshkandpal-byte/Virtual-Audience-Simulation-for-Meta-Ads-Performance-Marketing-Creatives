# Meta Ads Industry Strategy Playbook

This playbook is used by the app and its reusable AI-agent skills. These are starting hypotheses, not universal winning formulas. Adapt to actual product/service, audience, funnel stage, offer, geography, landing page, policies and live campaign evidence.

## Automated workflow
1. Inventory creative, headline/hook, copy/script, offer, CTA, objective, audience and destination URL.
2. Infer industry only from supplied facts or accessible page text; include confidence and evidence. If uncertain, use general strategy.
3. Review landing-page title, headings, description, visible text, offer, CTA and trust information when safe and reachable. Report unavailable checks honestly.
4. Apply the matching industry strategy and format-specific checklist.
5. Simulate cold/problem-aware, warm/solution-aware and high-intent reactions as hypotheses, not observed human responses.
6. Recommend specific edits to hook, heading, copy, offer, CTA, visual execution and landing page.
7. If an AI provider is configured, draft up to three differentiated variants based only on verified facts; otherwise return local guidance and disclose the limitation.
8. Produce valid JSON, risk flags, evidence gaps and a prioritized test plan. Never publish or launch campaigns automatically.

## Funnel and objective guidance
- TOFU/cold: relevant hook, problem/use case, simple explanation or useful education; avoid high commitment before trust.
- MOFU/warm: demos, FAQs, comparisons, process, genuine reviews/case evidence and objection handling.
- BOFU/high intent: exact offer, price/availability, proof, shipping/returns/booking terms and low-friction CTA.
- Leads: explain what happens after submission and keep forms proportionate.
- WhatsApp: clarify what users can ask and next step; response-time claims must be verified.
- Traffic/awareness/engagement: match CTA to intent; engagement is not proof of purchase intent.

## Copy and offer principles
- Hook: relevant problem, true demonstration, specific use case, reveal or useful question.
- Heading: one clear, concrete, audience-relevant message.
- Primary text: hook → context/problem → verified benefit/mechanism → proof/detail → offer/terms → CTA; adapt as appropriate.
- Offer: bundle, convenience, pricing, consultation, trial, guarantee or shipping only if real and documented.
- CTA: match objective and destination (shop to product page, book to booking page, message to messaging destination).
- Do not assume video, UGC, static or carousel always wins. Test based on product, audience, evidence and execution.

## Landing-page audit
Review reachability and extracted content; ad-to-page message/offer match; headline clarity; product/service and price/terms; CTA; trust information; friction and missing details. Do not claim mobile usability, checkout functionality or page speed was tested unless directly measured.

## Evidence labels
- Observed: present in uploaded creative or accessible page text.
- Inferred: plausible interpretation with confidence and evidence.
- Hypothesis: predicted reaction/strategy to test, not measured.
- Unknown: missing or inaccessible; do not guess.

## Integrity and regulated categories
Never fabricate product facts, prices, testimonials, certifications, client logos, results, scarcity, deadlines, stock, delivery times or guarantees. Use additional caution for healthcare, supplements/Ayurveda, finance, housing, employment and legal services. Do not infer sensitive personal attributes. Do not claim Meta policy approval.
